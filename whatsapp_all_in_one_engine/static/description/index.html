Go Live in About 60 Seconds

  1. Install Module: Install whatsapp_all_in_one_engine into your Odoo 17, 18, or 19 database.
  2. Add Number: Go to WhatsApp Engine > Accounts & QR Setup, click New, enter your number and click Generate QR Code.
